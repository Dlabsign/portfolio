### Smart Fending Machine

This project focused on designing a modern, fresh, and intuitive UI/UX for a mobile application for online ordering of fruit juices and salads. The goal was to create a seamless ordering experience for health-conscious customers. The app's design uses a clean aesthetic with a dominant bright green color palette to evoke a sense of freshness and healthy living.

This project was developed as part of the Jakarta International Science Fair and was awarded a Gold Medal, recognizing its innovation, design quality, and user-centered approach.
