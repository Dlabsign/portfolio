### Movie Ticketing Mobile App

This project focuses on designing a mobile UI/UX for a cinema ticket booking application. The primary goal is to create a seamless, intuitive, and fast user experience—from browsing movies to completing the checkout process.

The design adopts a dark mode aesthetic combined with a distinctive orange accent to reflect the brand identity. This approach not only delivers a modern and cinematic feel but also helps reduce eye strain, especially when users access the app in low-light environments such as cinema areas.
