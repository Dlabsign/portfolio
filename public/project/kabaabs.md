### Kabaabs

This project focuses on the user interface and user experience (UI/UX) design, as well as the website development for a kebab restaurant platform that offers delicious, fast, and affordable street food. The primary target audience is young adults, students, and busy individuals who are looking for quick, tasty, and satisfying meals.

The main focus of this website design is to provide easy access to menu information, intuitive navigation for ordering, and a visually engaging layout that highlights the freshness and appeal of the food. The design aims to create a modern and appetizing experience that encourages users to explore the menu and place orders effortlessly.
